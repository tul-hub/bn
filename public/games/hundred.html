How to play

  
Goal. Four players share 16 cards (25, 30 … 100). Collect at least as many points as you bid, round after round.

  
Card points. A card is worth its tens digit: 25 is 2, 30 and 35 are 3 … 100 is 10. The 25 is the weakest card, called পঁচা. Every round holds 96 points.

  
Bidding. Look at your four cards and secretly bid 3 to 30 points. All bids are revealed together.

  
Tricks. Whoever holds the 25 (পঁচা) opens the first trick of every round, and that card is played automatically. Then everyone plays one card clockwise. There are no suits: the biggest number wins all four cards and leads the next trick. Four tricks make a round.

  
Scoring. If the points you collected reach your bid, you score them. If not, you score 0 for the round. The host can switch to scoring the bid itself.

  
Winning. Play 5, 10 or 15 rounds, or race to 100 total points. The highest total wins, and a tie plays one more round.
