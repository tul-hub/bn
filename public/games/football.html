How to play

  
Goal. Score more than the other team before the clock runs out. Keepers are controlled by the computer.

  
Move. Drag on the left half of the screen (or use WASD / arrow keys). Hold SPRINT (Shift) to run faster; it drains stamina.

  
With the ball. PASS (Z) sends it to a team-mate in the direction you are moving. Hold SHOOT (Space) to charge power, release to shoot.

  
Without the ball. TACKLE (Z) slides in to steal it. SWITCH (X) hands you the team-mate nearest the ball.

  
Teams. Players join Red and Blue alternately. Any empty spot is played by the computer, and takes over if someone leaves.
