How to play

  
Goal. Capture all sixteen of the other side's beads, or leave them with no legal move. Red starts from the bottom, blue from the top.

  
Moving. Tap one of your beads, then tap a glowing green point. A bead slides one step along a line to an empty point, in any direction. Diagonals only exist where the lines are drawn.

  
Capturing. Jump over an adjacent rival bead along a line into the empty point right behind it. The jumped bead is removed. Captures are optional.

  
Chain jumps. After a capture, the same bead may keep jumping if another capture is open. Tap the next red point, or tap End move to stop.

  
Quiet game. If 80 turns pass without a capture, the player with more beads wins. Equal beads is a draw.
