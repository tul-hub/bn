How to play

Goal. Knock rivals off the stage or drain their health. Last fighter or team standing wins the round. Hits at low health launch much farther.

Parry. Block just as a hit lands to stun your attacker and slow time.

Special. Fires a blade wave. With the hammer it slams the ground. It recharges.

Crates. Grab falling crates for a spear, hammer, daggers or pistol. They wear out and return you to the blade.

Duo teams. Partners share a side and cannot hurt each other. The last team with a fighter standing wins.

Local network. Run node server.js on one computer, open the address it prints on every device, then host and join with the room code. Without the server, two tabs of the same browser can still play each other.

Players 3 and 4 on one device need gamepads. Touch controls appear automatically on phones.
