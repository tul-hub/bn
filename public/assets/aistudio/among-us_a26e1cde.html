How to play

Crewmates finish tasks at the glowing yellow consoles (hold USE). Impostors stalk the crew in the dark and use KILL. Find a body, hit REPORT, then vote someone out. If you die you become a ghost and can spectate any player, fly around freely, or watch through their eyes. Pick Spectator as your role to simply watch a match.

Keyboard: WASD or arrows move · E use · Q report · F kill · Tab / ← → switch spectated player · Esc menu
Touch: the stick on the left moves, buttons on the right.
LAN: open the LAN tab to host or join a match with friends on the same network.
