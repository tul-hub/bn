How to play

  
Goal. Move all four pieces around the board and into the centre before anyone else.

  
Leaving the yard. Roll a 6 to bring a piece onto your start arrow.

  
Extra rolls. A 6, a capture, or a piece reaching the centre gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row lose the turn.

  
Capture. Land on a rival piece to send it back to its yard. Starred and start squares are safe.

  
Finishing. Your coloured lane needs an exact roll to reach the centre.

  
Duo teams. Partners sit opposite. Once your pieces are home, you move your partner's. The first team with all eight home wins.
