How to play

  
Goal. Capture all of the other side's pieces, or leave them with no legal move. Red starts from the bottom and moves first.

  
Moving. Tap one of your pieces, then tap a highlighted square. Pieces slide one square diagonally forward onto an empty dark square.

  
Capturing. Jump over a rival piece diagonally into the empty square behind it. If another jump is available from where you land, you must keep going. With forced captures on, you have to jump whenever you can.

  
Kings. A piece that reaches the far row is crowned and ends its turn there. Kings move and jump forward and backward.

  
Draws. If 40 moves each pass with no capture and no uncrowned piece moving, the game is a draw.
