How to play

  
Goal. Pocket all nine coins of your colour before the other side does. White plays from the bottom, black from the opposite edge.

  
Shooting. Slide the striker along your baseline, then drag back anywhere on the board and let go. The farther you pull, the harder the shot. The dotted line shows where it will go.

  
Keep going. Pocket one of your own coins and you shoot again. A rival coin you pocket counts for them and ends your turn.

  
The queen. Pocket the red queen, then cover it by pocketing one of your own coins on the same or next shot. Otherwise it goes back to the centre. Your last coin cannot go in before the queen.

  
Fouls. If the striker falls in a pocket, the coins from that shot go back and you return one of your earlier coins.

  
Doubles. Four players, two teams. Partners sit opposite each other and share a colour.
